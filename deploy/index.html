Rules of the competition

  • Predict scores — Submit your predictions for each World Cup 2026 match before kickoff.
  • Earn points — Exact score = 3 pts, correct outcome = 1 pt, wrong = 0 pts.
  • Weekly rankings — Leaderboard resets each week. Top predictors earn TON rewards.
  • TON rewards — Prizes are distributed in TON cryptocurrency to your connected wallet.
  • Fair play — One account per person. Predictions lock at match kickoff.
