About

Rules of the competition

How to play

  • Predict — Pick the winner and exact score for each World Cup 2026 match.
  • Earn points — 1 point for the correct outcome, 3 points for the exact score.
  • Deadline — Predictions lock at each match's kickoff (UTC). Late predictions are not accepted.

Prize

For each World Cup 2026 matchweek (a matchweek = a set of 10 matches as scheduled by Betty), Betty awards 1 TON to one winner.

Winner determination

  1. Highest total points from that matchweek's matches.
  2. Tie-break 1: earliest timestamp of the player's last submitted prediction for that matchweek.
  3. Tie-break 2: lowest Telegram user ID.

Rules

  • Free to enter. No purchase necessary.
  • Payout. Winners are announced within 24 hours of the matchweek's last final whistle and paid in TON to the Telegram Wallet linked to the winning Telegram account.
  • Taxes. Recipients are responsible for any taxes in their jurisdiction.
  • Eligibility. Void where prohibited by local law.

Betty is a free skill-based prediction contest. Winners are determined by prediction accuracy, not chance. Prizes have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money outside the TON network.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 April 2026

Who we are

Betty (the “Service”) is a free prediction game for top world football matches, available as a Telegram Mini App via @bettyscores_bot and at app.bettyscores.com.

Data we collect

  • Telegram account data — your Telegram user ID, first name, last name (if set), and username (if set). Provided to us by Telegram when you open the Mini App.
  • Your predictions — the scores you submit for matches and their submission timestamps.
  • Score and ranking — your accumulated points and leaderboard position, derived from your predictions.
  • TON wallet address — only if you win a prize, and only the address you provide for payout.
  • Technical data — standard web-server logs (IP address, user agent, request timestamps), retained briefly for security and abuse prevention.

Why we collect it

  • To run the prediction game (scoring, leaderboard, prize payouts).
  • To display your name on the leaderboard so other players can see results.
  • To pay TON prizes to winners.
  • To prevent abuse and protect the Service.

Who we share data with

  • Telegram — we run inside Telegram and use Telegram’s SDK; their Privacy Policy applies.
  • Cloudflare — we host the Service on Cloudflare Pages and Workers. Cloudflare processes requests on our behalf as a data processor.
  • The TON network — if you win a prize, your TON wallet address and the payout transaction become publicly visible on the TON blockchain (this is inherent to public blockchains and cannot be undone).
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Predictions and leaderboard records are kept for the duration of the active season plus up to 12 months for historical records. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days. Wallet addresses associated with completed payouts are kept indefinitely as part of the payment ledger.

Your rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated predictions at any time by contacting @bettyscores_bot. Note that public leaderboard records and on-chain TON payout transactions cannot be removed retroactively.

Cookies and tracking

The Service uses local storage to remember your Telegram session inside the Mini App. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use the Service.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be announced in the Mini App.

Contact
