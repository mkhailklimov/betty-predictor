About

Rules of the competition

How to play

  • Predict — Pick the winner and exact score for each World Cup 2026 match.
  • Earn points — 1 point for the correct outcome, 3 points for the exact score.
  • Deadline — Predictions lock at each match's kickoff (UTC). Late predictions are not accepted.

Prize

For each World Cup 2026 matchweek (a matchweek = a set of 10 matches as scheduled by Betty), Betty awards 1 TON to one winner.

Winner determination

  1. Highest total points from that matchweek's matches.
  2. Tie-break 1: earliest timestamp of the player's last submitted prediction for that matchweek.
  3. Tie-break 2: lowest Telegram user ID.

Rules

  • Free to enter. No purchase necessary.
  • Payout. Winners are announced within 24 hours of the matchweek's last final whistle. To receive the prize, the winner connects a TON wallet on the Leaderboard page in the Mini App; payment is sent on-chain to that address.
  • Taxes. Recipients are responsible for any taxes in their jurisdiction.
  • Eligibility. Void where prohibited by local law.

Betty is a free skill-based prediction contest. Winners are determined by prediction accuracy, not chance. Prizes have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money outside the TON network.

Privacy Policy

Betty stores your Telegram user ID, your Telegram username (if you have one), and your predictions to run the game and display the leaderboard. If you win a prize, we also store the TON wallet address you provide for payout — the payout transaction is publicly visible on the TON blockchain. We don’t collect your first or last name. We don’t sell or share your data with advertisers. The Service is hosted on Cloudflare; Telegram’s Privacy Policy also applies inside the Mini App. You can request deletion of your account and predictions at any time via @bettyscores_bot; public leaderboard records and on-chain payouts cannot be removed retroactively.
