# Club crest prompts v3 (esports mascot style, approved 17 Nov)

Style approved by the owner on the Man Utd red devil (done, `EPL_MAN.png`). Subject first, a bust where the club has a creature, subject large and inside the shield.
Our own emblems: not copies of the official crests, no names or letters.
Save each picture as `betty-release-2.1/frontend/public/teams/Cards/<CODE>.png`; I cut the background (`C:/Users/mihel/betty-crests/cut.py`) and wire it in.
